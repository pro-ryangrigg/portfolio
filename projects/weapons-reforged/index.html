Weapons Reforged

Modding • WeiDU • Baldur’s Gate EE/EET • Systems Design

Project Overview

Weapons Reforged is a modular, data‑driven overhaul of weapon usability and proficiency rules for Baldur’s Gate Enhanced Editions and EET. It provides a clean, compatibility‑focused framework for universal weapon access, proficiency minimums, class‑restricted proficiency caps, and optional removal of fighting style limits — all without altering item descriptions, weapon stats, or kit definitions.

The mod is designed for long‑term stability, interoperability with large mod stacks, and ease of translation and maintenance. It is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

My Role

  • Designed the modular WeiDU architecture
  • Developed all item‑level and proficiency‑level patching logic
  • Ensured compatibility with BGEE, BG2EE, and EET
  • Created translation‑ready component strings
  • Maintained documentation and community support

Tools & Technologies

  • WeiDU — mod installer and patching framework
  • Infinity Engine Enhanced Editions — BGEE, BG2EE, EET
  • 2DA Table Patching — clasweap.2da, weapprof.2da, etc.
  • Item Usability Byte Patching
  • PRETTY_PRINT_2DA for clean output
  • Translation System for multilingual support

Key Features

  • Item‑level usability overhauls with alignment preservation
  • Universal weapon access (Monks excluded)
  • Minimum proficiency options (1–5 pips)
  • Enhanced proficiency caps respecting class restrictions
  • Fighting style cap removal for all classes except Monks and Kensai
  • Modular, conflict‑resistant component design

Technical Challenges & Solutions

  • Clean WeiDU architecture: Solved with component isolation, REQUIRE_PREDICATE checks, and safe patching.
  • Auto‑detection of engine structures: Automatically detects MONK/KENSAI columns and weapon row ranges.
  • Compatibility with large mod stacks: Avoids altering stats or descriptions; patches only usability and proficiency tables.

Results & Impact

Weapons Reforged provides a stable, predictable overhaul of weapon usability and proficiency rules. It is fully compatible with BGEE, BG2EE, and EET, supports nine languages, and is widely used within the Baldur’s Gate modding community. The modular design ensures long‑term maintainability and interoperability with large mod stacks.
